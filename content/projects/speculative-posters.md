This assignment was designed for undergraduate course titled Principles of Visual design taught to students at the school of Literature Media and Communication at the Georgia Institute of Technology. The assignment consisted of designing a public awareness poster about an imaginary social, technological, political or environmental issue in the future. Students had to not only use futures methods to imagine future world but also express their ideas and narrative in a visually compelling way, utilizing practical and theoretical design skills taught in the course. Role: instructor.
