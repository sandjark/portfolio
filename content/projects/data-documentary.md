This project is a data documentary about the real estate politics and economics in Atlanta. A data documentary is a non-fiction, interactive representation in which data is used to convey an insight or story. We used building and demolition permit data from the City of Atlanta to reveal stories about gentrification in the city. The documentary can be accessed here (http://jagodwin.com/data_doc/english-module.html). Role: design researcher. Collaborators: Jessica Anderson, Alex Godwin
