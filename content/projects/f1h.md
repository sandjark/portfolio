Futures in 1 Hour (F1H) is participatory toolkit for futures literacy. It is designed to introduce participants to some of the basic concepts of futures studies including scenario development, stakeholder analysis, values and others. The tool also encourages the use of design and visual expression to speculate about possible implication of future scenarios. The toolkit is available for download under Creative Commons license. The toolkit has been presented at a number of conferences and in educational institutions including Georgia Institute of Technology and Parson's School of Design. Role: principal designer, futures researcher, educator
